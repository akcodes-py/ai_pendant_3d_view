Stack Architecture Breakdown

● Starboard 1 (Front Deck): Mic + SD + Controls

Directly beneath the front cardboard cover. Houses:
• Upper-Side INMP441 Mic directly behind the top acoustic hole.
• 5mm Blue Indicator LED with dome extending through the front.
• Round Push Button for thumb activation / AI trigger.
• ON/OFF Power Slide Switch to turn the entire device on and off.
• MicroSD card reader on the side edge for direct card insertion.

● Starboard 2 (Middle Deck): ESP32-S3

Centrally positioned microcontroller tier. Mounts the ESP32-S3 DevKit (70 × 39 × 10 mm) with dual header pin rows routing signals upward to Deck 1 and power downward to Deck 3.

● Starboard 3 (Bottom Deck): Power Subsystem

Houses the MT3608 5V Step-Up Boost Converter (45 × 23 × 15 mm), TP4056 Charger (30 × 18 × 3 mm) with USB-C connector facing the side cutout, and securely beds the 37 × 25 × 5 mm LiPo battery.
